osu! Beatmap Downloader

Bulk-download beatmaps in the background, then import them into osu! in one click.

Every beatmap is downloaded straight from osu.ppy.sh, the official osu! website, using your own account. No third-party mirrors or other download sites are used.

Is this safe?
You sign in on osu.ppy.sh in your own browser, including any captcha or email code osu! asks for. The app doesn't control that tab or read what you type. When you're done it copies just your osu.ppy.sh session cookie out of your browser into its own, so downloads can use it. No other site's cookies are read.

If your browser keeps its cookies encrypted (anything Chromium-based), the app instead opens its own Chrome window on a private profile, and the session stays in there.

Your sign-in is kept in this app's data folder (it lasts about a month), so don't share that folder. Sign out removes it. The app is open source.

Downloading

Download from i Mirrors are community servers that host the same .osz files. They need no sign-in and have no hourly limit, so they're roughly ten times faster.

osu.ppy.sh downloads through a hidden browser using your own account, exactly as if you clicked Download on the site. It's subject to osu!'s ~200/hour limit.

Heads-up: osu! allows about 200 beatmap downloads per hour (more with osu!supporter). Because every map comes from osu.ppy.sh, the app sticks to that limit. When you reach it, the app waits and retries automatically after 5, 10, 20 and 25 minutes, then keeps going, so big batches take roughly an hour per 200 maps.
